instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Whisk Marinade & Coat"
    text: "In a mixing bowl, whisk together the dahi, mustard oil, Kashmiri chili, garam masala, roasted cumin, ginger-garlic paste, crushed kasuri methi, and salt until thick and smooth. Gently fold in paneer cubes, capsicum, and onion petals. Let marinate for 15 minutes."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Heat Skillet to High"
    text: "Place your cast iron skillet or heavy iron tawa over a high gas flame until lightly smoking. Add 1 tbsp ghee and swirl to coat the surface evenly."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 3
    title: "Sear Paneer Without Crowding"
    text: "Place paneer cubes and vegetables in a single uncrowded layer. Do not disturb for 2-3 minutes so a dark tandoori-style char crust develops. Turn with tongs and sear remaining sides for another 2 minutes."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 4
    title: "Toss & Serve Sizzling"
    text: "Toss everything together for 30 seconds so aromatic roasted spices coat the peppers. Take pan off the burner immediately to ensure the paneer stays silky soft. Dust generously with chaat masala and fresh lemon juice directly in the skillet."
    durationMinutes: 2
