instructions:

    title: "Temper Aromatics & Make Roux"
    text: "Melt butter in your skillet over medium heat. Add jeera, minced garlic, and slit green chilies. Sauté 30-40 seconds until fragrant. Sprinkle in maida and whisk continuously for 1 minute to cook out the raw flour smell."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 2
    title: "Whisk in Milk until Glossy"
    text: "Slowly pour in warm milk in a steady stream while whisking briskly to prevent lumps. Simmer on medium-low flame for 3-4 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon."
