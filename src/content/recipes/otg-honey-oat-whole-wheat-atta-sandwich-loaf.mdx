instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Make the Boiling-Milk Scald"
    text: "In a mixing bowl, combine 35g oats and 35g chakki atta. Pour 140ml boiling hot milk over them and whisk vigorously into a thick, glossy porridge. Cover and let sit for 15 minutes until cooled to lukewarm (<38°C / 100°F). This scald neutralizes sharp wheat bran that otherwise cuts gluten strands."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Mix & Bran Hydration Autolyse"
    text: "To the cooled oat-atta scald, add lukewarm water (105ml), honey, instant yeast, and whisked room-temperature dahi. Add remaining 165g chakki atta, 135g maida, optional vital wheat gluten, optional milk powder, and salt. Stir with a spoon into a rough dough, cover, and rest for 15 minutes to allow dense whole-wheat bran to hydrate thoroughly."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 3
    title: "Knead in Butter & Toasted Sesame"
    text: "Spread softened (or cooled browned) Amul butter and optional toasted sesame seeds over the dough. Knead on an ungreased counter for 9 to 10 minutes. The dough will transition from tacky to elastic, smooth, and supple, passing the poke test with resilient spring."
    durationMinutes: 10
  - step: 4
    title: "First Bulk Rise"
    text: "Shape dough into a smooth round ball. Place in a lightly greased bowl, cover tightly with a damp tea towel or lid, and proof until doubled in volume (45 minutes in warm kitchen >28°C / 82°F; 65 minutes in cool weather)."
    durationMinutes: 45
    summerDurationMinutes: 45
    winterDurationMinutes: 65
  - step: 5
    title: "Shape with Surface Tension"
    text: "Gently punch down dough. Flatten into an 8x12-inch rectangle, pressing out large air bubbles. Roll up tightly from the short edge like a Swiss roll, pinching the seam firmly after each roll to build strong surface tension. Brush the top with milk, roll in the optional oat-sesame seed mix, and place seam-side down in a greased 9x4-inch anodized aluminium loaf tin."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 6
    title: "Second Proof in Loaf Tin"
    text: "Cover the tin loosely with a damp cloth. Let rise until the highest point of the crown arches 1 inch above the rim of the loaf tin (35 minutes in warm weather, 45 minutes in cool weather)."
    durationMinutes: 35
    summerDurationMinutes: 35
    winterDurationMinutes: 45
  - step: 7
    title: "OTG Bake at 180°C (356°F) & Foil-Tent Cue"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 180°C (356°F) with Both Rods On (Fan Off) for a full 15 minutes. Place the loaf tin on the lower-middle rack and bake for 28 to 32 minutes until the loaf sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom. Check color at the 18-minute mark: if the honey-oat crown is already deep golden, lay a loose sheet of aluminium foil over the top to prevent scorching. Turn out immediately onto a wire cooling rack and cool completely for 1 hour before slicing so the whole-wheat crumb sets cleanly."
    durationMinutes: 30
notes:

  - "Slice Cooling Rule: Never slice warm whole-wheat bread—the steam inside needs to redistribute into the starch network; cutting early results in a gummy texture."
