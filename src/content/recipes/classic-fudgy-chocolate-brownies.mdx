instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Prep the Tin & True OTG Preheat"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 180°C (350°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On. Grease an 11×7-inch (28×18 cm) rectangular tin (or a 9×9-inch square pan) with butter. Line with parchment paper, leaving 2-inch overhangs on opposite sides to serve as a sling for easy lifting later."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Melt Butter & Chocolate (Bain-Marie)"
    text: "Place 225g diced butter and 150g chopped dark chocolate into a heatproof bowl. Set the bowl over a small saucepan of gently simmering water (ensure the bottom of the bowl does not touch the water). Stir with a spatula until completely melted, glossy, and smooth. Remove bowl from heat and let cool for 3 to 4 minutes until warm to the touch."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 3
    title: "Combine Dry Flour & Sugar"
    text: "In a large bowl, sift together 225g maida, 1.5 tsp baking powder, and 0.25 tsp salt (replicating self-raising flour). Add 125g dark brown sugar (or muscovado) and whisk well to break up any dense sugar clumps."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 4
    title: "Emulsify Eggs into Chocolate"
    text: "Gradually stir the 4 beaten eggs (and optional 1 tsp vanilla) into the warm melted chocolate-butter mixture, whisking briskly until fully incorporated and glossy."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 5
    title: "Fold Wet into Dry"
    text: "Pour the chocolate-egg mixture into the dry flour bowl. Fold gently with a silicone spatula just until the flour is absorbed and a thick, luscious chocolate batter forms."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 6
    title: "Fold in Hazelnuts, Sultanas & Choco Chips"
    text: "Add 60g chopped hazelnuts (or walnuts), 60g sultanas, and 100g dark chocolate chips to the batter. Fold gently until evenly distributed throughout the mixture."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 7
    title: "Fill the Tin & Level"
    text: "Spoon the batter evenly into the prepared rectangular tin. Smooth and level the surface with the back of an offset spatula."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 8
    title: "OTG Bake for Fudgy Perfection"
    text: "Bake on the middle rack at 180°C (350°F) for 28 to 30 minutes. Tent loosely with foil at 20 minutes if the top surface is already glossy and crisp. The golden brownie doneness rule: Insert a wooden toothpick into the center—it should come out with moist, sticky crumbs clinging to it, NOT bone-dry clean. If clean, the brownie has turned into dry cake."
    durationMinutes: 30
  - step: 9
    title: "Cool & Settle"
    text: "Remove tin from OTG and let rest on a counter wire rack for 15 minutes. Using the parchment sling, carefully lift the brownie slab out of the tin and place onto the wire rack to cool completely to room temperature (about 45 minutes)."
    durationMinutes: 45
  - step: 10
    title: "White Chocolate Drizzle & Slice"
    text: "Melt 115g white chocolate in a bowl set over warm water (or microwave in 20-second bursts at 50% power). Spoon into a small piping bag or use a fork to drizzle fine zig-zag lines across the cooled brownie slab. Allow drizzle to set for 15 minutes, then slice into 15 generous squares using a sharp knife dipped in warm water."
    durationMinutes: 15
notes:
  - "Indian Self-Raising Flour Hack: Self-raising flour is simply 225g maida whisked with 1.5 tsp baking powder and a pinch of salt. This provides the exact delicate lift while keeping the brownie centers dense and fudgy."
  - "The Fudgy Brownie Doneness Secret: Traditional cake recipes instruct testing until a skewer comes out completely clean. For authentic brownies, a toothpick inserted into the center MUST come out with moist crumbs. Residual heat during cooling solidifies the molten chocolate into pure fudge."
  - "Nut Substitutions: Blanched hazelnuts provide authentic European crunch, but chopped Indian walnuts (akhrot) or cashews (kaju) taste sensational and are readily available at every local kirana."
  - "Eggless Swap: Replace 4 eggs with 120g (0.5 cup) fresh thick dahi (curd) whisked with 60ml (0.25 cup) warm milk and 1 tbsp cornflour."
  - "Storage: Store brownie squares in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days, or refrigerate for up to 7 days. Cold brownies develop a chewy, truffle-like texture."
