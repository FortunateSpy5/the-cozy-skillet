instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Mix the Sticky Shaggy Dough"
    text: "In a large bowl, whisk lukewarm water, yeast, honey, and optional dahi until dissolved. Add maida, chakki atta, optional vital wheat gluten, salt, dried herbs, and pepper/chilli flakes. Stir with a wooden spoon or wet hand for 2 minutes into a wet, shaggy dough ball without dry flour pockets. Cover and let rest undisturbed for 20 minutes."
    durationMinutes: 4
  - step: 2
    title: "Incorporate Garlic & Cheese with Stretch-and-Folds"
    text: "Wet your hands with clean water. Scatter cooled roasted garlic cloves and optional cheddar cubes over the dough. Reach under one side, stretch the dough up, and fold it over the center. Rotate the bowl 90 degrees and repeat 4 times around the bowl to fold in the aromatics. Cover, rest 20 minutes, and perform one more set of 4 stretch-and-folds until the dough tightens and feels elastic."
    durationMinutes: 30
  - step: 3
    title: "Bulk Fermentation"
    text: "Cover tightly. Let rise at room temperature for 50 minutes (warm kitchen >28°C / 82°F) or 75 minutes (cool room) until puffy, jiggly, and covered in surface bubbles. (For 10x deeper sourdough complexity, place covered bowl in the refrigerator for 10 to 14 hours for slow cold fermentation)."
    durationMinutes: 50
    summerDurationMinutes: 50
    winterDurationMinutes: 75
  - step: 4
    title: "Shape the Round Artisan Boule"
    text: "Dust your counter lightly with flour. Tip the puffy dough out gently to preserve interior bubbles. Fold the four corners into the center like an envelope, flip seam-side down, and cup both hands around the dough. Pull gently toward your body across the counter in quarter-turns to create a taut, smooth surface skin. Transfer onto a parchment-lined baking sheet or 8-inch round cake tin dusted with fine sooji."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 5
    title: "Final Proof & Scoring"
    text: "Cover loosely with an inverted large bowl so the dough doesn't stick to a towel. Proof for 35 minutes until plump and airy (30 minutes in warm kitchen, 45 minutes in cool kitchen). Dust the top lightly with flour and slash a 1 cm deep cross ('+') across the crown with a sharp serrated bread knife or razor blade to guide maximum oven spring expansion."
    durationMinutes: 35
    summerDurationMinutes: 30
    winterDurationMinutes: 45
  - step: 6
    title: "OTG Steam-Bake (Phase 1: Steam Spring & Phase 2: Crisp)"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 220°C (428°F) with Both Rods On (Fan Off) for 15 minutes with an empty small metal tray on the bottom floor. Slide the scored loaf onto the middle rack. Carefully pour 1 cup of boiling water into the hot bottom tray to create instant steam, immediately shut the door, and adjust dial to 210°C (410°F). Bake with steam for 15 minutes (Phase 1: Steam Spring). Then carefully remove the water tray with oven mitts and bake dry for another 15 to 17 minutes (Phase 2: Dry Crisp) until the crust is deep mahogany and crackles audibly when tapped. Cool for 30 minutes on a wire rack before slicing."
    durationMinutes: 32
notes:
  - "Indian Kitchen Note: Amul cheddar block or processed cheese cubes hold shape nicely without leaking excessively; if an oven-safe Dutch oven is unavailable, use a heavy stainless steel handi with a flat metal lid or the inverted cake tin steam method."
  - "The OTG Steam Trick: Tabletop OTGs vent air rapidly, which often makes bread crusts tough. Generating steam with a hot metal pan during the first 15 minutes gelatinizes exterior starches, producing a paper-thin, blistered, glass-crisp crust without a Dutch oven."
  - "Cheese Cubes vs Grated: Always use 1 cm cubes of cheddar rather than grated cheese. Grated cheese dissolves into the dough and weighs it down; cubes melt into pockets of molten cheese."
  - "Storage: Slice and freeze leftover artisan bread. Reheats in a toaster or hot skillet to fresh-baked crispness."
