instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Prep Crust & Foil-Wrap the Springform"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 150°C (300°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On. Line the base of a 7-inch or 8-inch springform pan with parchment paper. Take two large sheets of 18-inch heavy-duty aluminium foil and wrap the outside of the pan securely, pressing the foil firmly against the sides and rim. This double-barrier ensures no water seeps through the springform base during the water bath bake."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Press & Chill the Biscuit Base"
    text: "In a bowl, mix 140g finely crushed digestive biscuit crumbs, 60g melted butter, 15g sugar, and salt until evenly moistened like wet sand. Transfer to the prepared pan and press firmly into an even, compact layer using the flat bottom of a drinking glass. Transfer the pan to the freezer for 10 minutes to set the butter firm while preparing the filling."
    durationMinutes: 10
  - step: 3
    title: "Beat the Cream Cheese Smooth"
    text: "In a mixing bowl, beat 400g softened cream cheese on low-medium speed for 2 minutes until completely smooth, creamy, and free of lumps. Scrape the sides of the bowl with a silicone spatula."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 4
    title: "Stream in Condensed Milk & Hung Curd"
    text: "Pour in 200g sweetened condensed milk and 120g thick hung curd. Beat on low speed for 1 to 2 minutes until glossy and uniform. Add 60ml Amul fresh cream, 1 tsp vanilla extract, and 1 tsp fresh lemon juice, mixing gently."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 5
    title: "Sift Cornstarch & Whisk Silky Smooth"
    text: "Sift 15g (1.5 tbsp) cornflour through a fine mesh sieve into the batter. Whisk on the lowest speed for 30 to 45 seconds just until incorporated. Do not over-beat to avoid whipping excess air bubbles into the emulsion."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 6
    title: "Assemble the Bain-Marie Water Bath"
    text: "Remove the springform pan from the freezer. Pour the silky cheesecake batter over the chilled crust and smooth the top with a spatula. Place the foil-wrapped pan inside a larger, deep roasting tray. Pour boiling water into the outer roasting tray until it reaches halfway up the sides of the springform pan (about 1 inch deep)."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 7
    title: "Gentle Steam-Bake in OTG"
    text: "Carefully slide the roasting pan onto the middle rack of the preheated OTG. Bake at 150°C (300°F) for 50 minutes with Both Rods On. If the top starts getting golden around 30 minutes, tent loosely with a sheet of aluminium foil. When done, the edges should be gently set, while the center 2 to 3 inches should have a delicate, subtle jiggle."
    durationMinutes: 50
  - step: 8
    title: "Slow In-Oven Cooling"
    text: "Turn off the OTG. Prop the oven door open slightly by 1 inch using a wooden spoon handle and let the cheesecake sit inside the cooling oven for 60 minutes. This slow cooling stabilizes the starch matrix and prevents sudden surface cracking."
    durationMinutes: 60
  - step: 9
    title: "Chill Firm Overnight"
    text: "Carefully remove the springform pan from the water bath, strip off the foil wrap, and let cool completely at room temperature for 30 minutes. Transfer to the refrigerator and chill uncovered for at least 6 hours, or ideally overnight (8 to 12 hours) until set firm. Run a thin knife around the inner rim, release the springform collar, and slice clean wedges."
    durationMinutes: 5
notes:
  - "The Science of Eggless Setting: The natural calcium and milk proteins in condensed milk bond with gelatinized cornstarch (Weikfield cornflour) to create a firm, sliceable custard matrix that mimics egg proteins without any rubbery texture or eggy odor."
  - "Water Bath Leak Protection: Springform pans are notoriously prone to microscopic water leaks. Using extra-wide (18-inch) heavy-duty foil ensures seamless coverage. Alternatively, place the springform pan inside a slightly larger 8-inch silicone cake mould before setting it in the water bath."
  - "Indian Pantry Sweetener: Nestlé Milkmaid or Amul Mithai Mate both work identically. The sweetness from 200g condensed milk is perfectly balanced by the natural lactic acidity of 120g hung curd and 1 tsp fresh lemon juice."

A foolproof, restaurant-quality eggless New York baked cheesecake. Powered by a delicate emulsion of D'lecta cream cheese, Amul condensed milk, and drained hung curd, it bakes under a gentle OTG Bain-Marie into an ivory-smooth, crack-free velvet custard over a crunchy digestive biscuit base.
