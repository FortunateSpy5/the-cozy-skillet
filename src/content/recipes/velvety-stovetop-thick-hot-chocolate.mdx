instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Whisk the Cold Slurry"
    text: "In a small cup or ramekin, combine 60ml (1/4 cup) of cold milk with the 1.5 tsp cornstarch. Whisk vigorously with a fork or mini whisk for 30 seconds until completely dissolved and free of chalky lumps. Set aside."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 2
    title: "Dissolve the Cocoa Paste"
    text: "In a small heavy-bottom saucepan or milk pan (patila), add the sifted cocoa powder, sugar, salt, and 60ml of the remaining milk. Place over low flame and whisk for 1 minute until a smooth, glossy chocolate paste forms. Gently warming cocoa powder with minimal liquid blooms its natural fat and roasted aromatics before the full liquid volume is added."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 3
    title: "Pour Milk & Heat"
    text: "Slowly pour in the remaining 360ml milk in a steady stream while whisking continuously to ensure no paste sticks to the corners of the pan. Turn flame to medium-low and heat until steam rises and tiny bubbles begin to form around the perimeter (around 75°C (167°F); do not let it boil vigorously)."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 4
    title: "Stream Slurry & Thicken"
    text: "Give the reserved cornstarch slurry a quick re-stir (starch settles to the bottom rapidly), then pour it into the steaming hot chocolate in a steady stream while whisking continuously. Keep the flame on low and simmer gently for 2 minutes, whisking constantly as the starch granules gelatinize, transforming the liquid into a glossy, velvety consistency that visibly coats the back of a spoon."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 5
    title: "Off-Heat Emulsion & Serve"
    text: "Remove saucepan from the heat. Immediately stir in vanilla extract, along with chopped dark chocolate or cold salted butter if using. Whisk for 30 seconds until the chocolate is fully melted and emulsified into a mirror-gloss ganache. Pour into warm ceramic mugs and enjoy immediately."
    durationMinutes: 1
notes:
  - "Indian Kitchen Note on Cornflour: In Indian grocery stores and quick commerce (Blinkit/Zepto), 'Cornflour' (Weikfield, Blue Bird) refers to fine white cornstarch, not yellow cornmeal (makki ka atta). White cornstarch is what gives this hot chocolate its signature Italian silkiness."
  - "Milk Fat Calibration: Amul Gold (full-cream, 6% fat) or fresh buffalo milk yields the creamiest result. If using toned milk (Amul Taaza), whisk in 1 tbsp of fresh malai or fresh dairy cream for richer mouthfeel."
  - "Reheating & Leftovers: This hot chocolate sets into a soft chocolate pudding when chilled in the fridge. To reheat, transfer back to a small saucepan over low flame and whisk in 2 to 3 tbsp of warm milk until smooth and flowing."
