instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Prep Tin & Preheat"
    text: "Preheat your OTG (or convection microwave) to 180°C (356°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On. Grease a 20–22 cm (8–8.5 inch) round cake tin with a little butter, line the base with butter paper, and grease the paper lightly."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Whisk Sifted Dry Ingredients"
    text: "In a medium bowl, whisk together 185g maida, 1.5 tsp baking powder, and 1/4 tsp salt. Whisk for 30 seconds to evenly distribute the leavener."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 3
    title: "Beat Butter & Sugar"
    text: "In a large mixing bowl, beat 170g butter and 110g powdered sugar with a hand whisk or electric beater for 2 minutes until pale, light, and fluffy."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 4
    title: "Add Eggs One by One"
    text: "Add the 3 eggs one by one, beating thoroughly for 30 seconds after each addition until each egg is fully incorporated into the fluffy emulsion. Beat in 1 tsp vanilla essence."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 5
    title: "Fold Flour & Milk into Batter"
    text: "Add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture along with 3 tbsp (45ml) lukewarm milk. Using a spatula, fold gently in a sweeping figure-eight motion just until smooth and no dry streaks remain (do not overmix). If using chocolate chips or tutti frutti, gently fold them in now."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 6
    title: "Bake in OTG (with Foil Shield Cue)"
    text: "Pour the batter into the prepared tin and smooth the top with a spatula. Place on the lower-middle or middle rack of your preheated 180°C (356°F) oven. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Note: If the top browns deeply before 25 minutes, tent loosely with a piece of aluminium foil and continue baking."
    durationMinutes: 35
  - step: 7
    title: "Cool & Slice"
    text: "Remove from the oven and let the cake rest in the tin for 10 minutes. Run a butter knife around the edge and invert onto a wire cooling rack. Peel off the butter paper and cool completely before slicing into clean wedges or tea-time fingers. Serve with hot chai or coffee."
    durationMinutes: 10
notes:
  - "The 3 Tbsp Milk Adjustment: The extra tablespoon of milk (45ml total) is essential for an 8-inch cake tin. It slightly relaxes the batter to ribbon consistency so the cake bakes with a flat, tender crumb rather than cracking open in the center."
  - "Convection Microwave Setting: If using an Indian convection microwave oven (LG, Samsung, IFB), place the tin on the low metal rack, press the Convection button, preheat to 180°C, and bake for 30–35 minutes."
  - "Foil Tent Cue: Because tea-time cakes contain rich butter and eggs, the top dome caramelizes rapidly under tabletop heating rods. If the dome reaches deep golden before 25 minutes, simply lay a loose sheet of foil on top to protect it while the center finishes setting."
  - "Storage: Keeps in an airtight cake container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerated for up to 7 days."
