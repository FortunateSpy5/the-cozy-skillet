instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Prep Skillet & Pre-Bake Crust"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 160°C (320°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On. Lightly butter the base and sides of a 9-inch or 10-inch seasoned cast iron skillet. In a bowl, combine 150g crushed digestive crumbs, 70g melted butter, 25g sugar, and a pinch of salt until the texture resembles wet sea sand. Press firmly and evenly across the bottom and 0.5-inch up the sides of the skillet using the flat base of a stainless steel glass. Bake the crust for 10 minutes until fragrant and toasted. Remove and let cool on a rack while making the filling."
    durationMinutes: 10
  - step: 2
    title: "Set Up the Oven Steam Rack"
    text: "Boil 3 cups (750ml) of water in a kettle. Pour the boiling water into a deep baking tray or cake tin and slide it onto the lowest bottom rack of the OTG. This creates a steamy, humid oven chamber that prevents surface cracking without having to submerge the cast iron skillet in water."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 3
    title: "Cream Cheese, Sugar & Hung Curd"
    text: "In a large bowl, beat 450g softened cream cheese and 150g castor sugar on medium-low speed for 2 minutes until glossy and perfectly lump-free. Add 120g thick hung curd (or sour cream) and beat on low speed for 1 minute until completely homogeneous."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 4
    title: "Incorporate Eggs on Low Speed"
    text: "Add eggs one at a time, mixing on the lowest speed just until each yolk blends into the batter. Scrape the sides and bottom of the bowl with a spatula after each addition to avoid hidden unmixed clumps."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 5
    title: "Fold in Flour, Vanilla & Lemon"
    text: "Sift in 20g (2 tbsp) maida, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 0.5 tsp fresh lemon zest, and 1 tsp fresh lemon juice. Whisk gently on low speed for 30 seconds until the batter is silky, thick, and pourable."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 6
    title: "Fill the Skillet & Bake"
    text: "Pour the velvety filling over the pre-baked digestive crust in the skillet. Smooth the top with a spatula. Place the skillet on the middle rack (directly above the steam tray on the bottom rack). Bake at 160°C (320°F) for 50 to 55 minutes. If the top begins browning around 35 minutes, tent loosely with a sheet of aluminium foil. The cheesecake is done when the outer 2 inches are set and slightly puffed, and the center 3 inches has a subtle, gentle jiggle."
    durationMinutes: 52
  - step: 7
    title: "The Door-Prop Gradual Cool (Crack Prevention)"
    text: "Turn off the OTG. Leave the door slightly ajar (prop open with a wooden spoon or folded kitchen towel) and let the skillet rest inside the cooling oven for 60 minutes. Gradual thermal cooling prevents sudden temperature shock that causes cheesecakes to split open."
    durationMinutes: 60
  - step: 8
    title: "Cool & Refrigerate Overnight"
    text: "Transfer the skillet to a counter wire rack to cool to room temperature (about 1 hour). Cover the skillet loosely with foil and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, or ideally overnight (8–12 hours), so the custard sets into a firm, sliceable velvet texture. Slice directly from the skillet with a warm, clean knife."
    durationMinutes: 5
notes:
  - "How to Make Instant Hung Curd (Chakka): Spoon 250g plain fresh dahi (Amul/Mother Dairy) into a clean muslin cloth or tea strainer set over a bowl. Let drain in the fridge for 2 to 3 hours until 120g of thick, Greek-style yogurt paste remains."
  - "Cast Iron Heat Retention: Cast iron radiates heat from bottom and sides with exceptional uniformity, giving the biscuit crust a deeply toasted, buttery crunch that stays crisp for days."
  - "Do Not Overmix Eggs: Whisking eggs at high speed incorporates air that expands in the oven and deflates into unsightly surface cracks. Always blend eggs on low speed."
  - "Storage: Keep the cheesecake in the skillet covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Warm individual slices at room temperature for 15 minutes before serving for maximum silkiness."

A showstopping New York classic reimagined for cast iron. Baked over a buttery McVitie's digestive biscuit crust, the velvety cream cheese filling is kissed with lemon zest and fresh hung curd for an authentic, tangy finish with a crack-free surface.
