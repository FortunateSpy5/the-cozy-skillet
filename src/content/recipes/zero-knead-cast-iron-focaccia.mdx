instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Mix the Sticky Shaggy Dough"
    text: "In a mixing bowl, whisk together lukewarm water, honey, yeast, 15ml olive oil, and optional dahi. Add maida and salt. Stir vigorously with a wooden spoon or wet hands for 1 to 2 minutes until no dry flour patches remain. The dough will look very wet, sticky, and shaggy—do not add extra flour."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 2
    title: "Two Quick Stretch-and-Folds"
    text: "Cover bowl with a damp cloth and rest for 15 minutes. Wet your fingers with water, reach under one side of the dough, stretch it upward, and fold it over the center. Rotate the bowl 90 degrees and repeat 4 times around the bowl. Cover, rest another 15 minutes, and perform one more set of 4 stretch-and-folds. The dough will instantly turn glossy, smooth, and bouncy."
    durationMinutes: 30
  - step: 3
    title: "Bulk Rise (Same-Day or Overnight Ferment)"
    text: "Cover tightly. For same-day baking: let rise at room temperature for 45 minutes until bubbly and nearly doubled. For maximal Italian bakery flavor: transfer covered bowl to the refrigerator for 12 to 18 hours—cold fermentation creates massive gluten bubbles and blistered crusts."
    durationMinutes: 45
    summerDurationMinutes: 40
    winterDurationMinutes: 60
  - step: 4
    title: "Transfer to Oiled Cast-Iron Skillet"
    text: "Pour 20g olive oil (or oil-butter blend) into a 10 or 12-inch seasoned cast iron skillet, coating the bottom and sides thoroughly. Gently tip the bubbly dough into the pan. Fold the dough once over itself and flip so both surfaces are slicked with oil. Let rest undisturbed for 35 minutes at room temperature (25 minutes in summer, 40 minutes in winter), gently nudging it with oily fingertips to naturally fill the skillet."
    durationMinutes: 35
    summerDurationMinutes: 25
    winterDurationMinutes: 40
  - step: 5
    title: "Brine, Dimple & Press Toppings"
    text: "In a small cup, whisk 15g oil with 20ml warm salted water until emulsified. Drizzle evenly over the dough. Oil your fingertips generously and press straight down into the dough until your fingers hit the cast iron pan bottom, wiggling gently to create deep crater dimples and huge air bubbles. Press caramelised onions, confit garlic cloves, fresh rosemary, and flaky sea salt deeply into the dimples so they don't scorch."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 6
    title: "High-Heat OTG Blister Bake at 210°C (410°F)"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 210°C (410°F) with Both Rods On (Fan Off) for a full 15 minutes. Place your baking pan or oven-safe skillet on the lowest rack slot directly above the bottom heating rod so radiant heat fries the olive-oil-and-butter base to a shattering crunch while the top rod blisters the brined dimples (22 to 25 minutes). Let rest in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer onto a wire rack to preserve the crunchy bottom crust before slicing into thick squares."
    durationMinutes: 24
notes:
