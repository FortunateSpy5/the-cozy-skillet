instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Cook & Cool the Tangzhong"
    text: "Whisk 20g Maida and 100ml water in a small saucepan over low heat for 2 to 3 minutes until it thickens into a glossy, pudding-like paste. Transfer to a small bowl, cover with cling wrap touching the surface to prevent skin formation, and cool to room temperature (or 10 minutes in the fridge)."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 2
    title: "Mix Base Dough & Rest"
    text: "Whisk 220g Maida, 12g Vital Wheat Gluten, 100g Chakki Atta, optional 15g milk powder, and 6g salt together while completely dry. In a separate large bowl, whisk lukewarm milk (140ml), cooled Tangzhong, 80g active sourdough starter, and 20g honey. Add the dry flour mix and combine into a shaggy ball with a spatula. Cover and rest for 30 minutes to autolyse."
    durationMinutes: 30
  - step: 3
    title: "Knead in Softened Amul Butter"
    text: "Flatten the rested dough slightly and smear 35g softened Amul butter across the surface. Knead on the counter (or inside the bowl using slap-and-fold motions) for 7 to 8 minutes until the butter is fully emulsified and the dough turns smooth, supple, and non-sticky."
    durationMinutes: 8
  - step: 4
    title: "Bulk Fermentation with 2 Stretch-and-Folds"
    text: "Transfer into a lightly greased bowl. Perform 1 set of stretch-and-folds at 45 minutes and a second set at 90 minutes. Let rise until puffy, dome-shaped, and increased in volume by 60% to 70% (about 2.5 hours total in a 28°C–30°C Indian kitchen). (Optional Cold Proof: You can refrigerate the bulk dough overnight for 8 to 12 hours for deeper flavor and shape cold the next morning)."
    durationMinutes: 150
  - step: 5
    title: "Shape for the Loaf Tin"
    text: "Pat the relaxed dough into an 8×12-inch rectangle on a lightly oiled counter. Roll tightly from the short edge into a neat cylinder, pinching the final bottom seam and ends firmly shut. If using the optional seed topping, brush the top lightly with milk and roll across the seeds. Place seam-side down into a greased 9×4-inch aluminium loaf tin."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 6
    title: "Tin Proof"
    text: "Cover the tin with an inverted bowl or tented greased wrap and proof undisturbed until the center of the dough dome crowns 1 inch above the rim of the tin."
    durationMinutes: 90
    summerDurationMinutes: 90
    winterDurationMinutes: 120
  - step: 7
    title: "OTG Bake & Butter Glaze"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 190°C with Both Rods On (Fan Off) for 15 minutes. Place the loaf tin on the lower-middle rack, turn the dial to 185°C, and bake for 30 to 34 minutes (tenting loosely with foil at 18 minutes once golden brown). Remove from oven and immediately brush the hot crown with melted Amul butter for a soft, glossy finish. Cool 1 hour on a wire rack before slicing."
    durationMinutes: 32
