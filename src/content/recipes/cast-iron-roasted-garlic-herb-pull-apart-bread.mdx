instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Bloom Garlic & Aromatics in Cast-Iron Skillet"
    text: "Melt 75g Amul butter in your 10-inch cast-iron skillet over lowest flame. Add minced garlic (35g) and sizzle gently for 3 minutes until fragrant, tender, and pale blond—never allow garlic to brown or turn acrid. Turn off heat. Immediately stir in oregano, chilli flakes, and optional kasuri methi so the warm fat blooms their essential oils. Scrape into a bowl, let cool to a spreadable consistency, then fold in chopped coriander. Leave a thin film of butter in the skillet, then dust the bottom evenly with 1 tbsp fine sooji (semolina) for an ultra-crisp base."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 2
    title: "Mix & Knead Pillowy Garlic Dough"
    text: "In a large bowl, whisk lukewarm milk-water, sugar, and yeast. Add maida, milk powder, garlic powder, salt, and 1.5 tbsp olive oil. Stir into a rough dough, then knead on a clean counter for 8 to 10 minutes until smooth, supple, and elastic."
    durationMinutes: 10
  - step: 3
    title: "First Bulk Rise"
    text: "Shape dough into a smooth ball. Place in an oiled bowl, cover tightly with a damp cloth or lid, and allow to rise until doubled in size (40 minutes in summer >28°C; 60 minutes in cooler kitchens)."
    durationMinutes: 45
    summerDurationMinutes: 40
    winterDurationMinutes: 60
  - step: 4
    title: "Roll, Layer & Assemble in Cast Iron"
    text: "Roll risen dough into a 12x16-inch rectangle on a lightly floured surface. Spread 75% of the cooled garlic herb butter evenly over the surface. Scatter grated cheese and optional jalapeños/corn evenly over the top. Cut into 16 squares (4x4 grid). Stack the squares into 4 piles of 4, then arrange them shingle-style standing on edge in a ring around the sooji-dusted cast-iron skillet. Reserve remaining garlic butter for after baking."
    durationMinutes: 10
  - step: 5
    title: "Second Proof in Skillet"
    text: "Cover the assembled skillet loosely with a clean tea towel and proof for 20 to 25 minutes until puffy. Lightly dab the top with a teaspoon of the remaining garlic butter."
    durationMinutes: 20
    summerDurationMinutes: 20
    winterDurationMinutes: 30
  - step: 6
    title: "Bake at 190°C & Stovetop Finish"
    text: "Bake in a preheated oven at 190°C (375°F) for 20 to 22 minutes until the edges are sizzling, deep golden brown, and the cheese is bubbling. Immediately brush the hot bread with the remaining garlic herb butter and dust with a pinch of chaat masala. (Stovetop Alternative: Place covered skillet on top of a flat tawa heat diffuser on low gas flame for 20 minutes until the bottom is crunchy and bread is fully baked)."
    durationMinutes: 22
notes:
  - "The Sooji Secret: Dusting fine semolina (sooji) directly onto the buttered cast-iron skillet creates a crisp, crunchy bottom crust reminiscent of Domino's garlic bread."
  - "Garlic Bitterness Fix: Never use raw garlic inside pull-apart bread. Poaching minced garlic gently in butter mellows the harsh allicin into sweet, rounded flavor."
  - "Make-Ahead: Assemble the bread in the skillet up to 12 hours ahead, cover tightly, and refrigerate. Bring to room temperature for 30 minutes before baking."
