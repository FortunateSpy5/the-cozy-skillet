instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Dry Whisk & True Autolyse"
    text: "In a large mixing bowl, thoroughly whisk 305g regular Maida, 15g Vital Wheat Gluten, and 80g Chakki Atta while completely dry. Pour in 280ml cool water (and optional 8g honey) and mix by hand until no dry flour bits remain. Cover and rest for 45 minutes—the VWG and Maida will self-assemble into extensible gluten strands without any kneading."
    durationMinutes: 45
  - step: 2
    title: "Incorporate Starter & Bassinage Salt Water"
    text: "Spread 80g peak sourdough starter over the rested dough. Wet your hand, dimple it in, and scoop-and-slap the dough inside the bowl (Rubaud method) for 3 minutes. Next, dissolve 8g salt in the 20ml reserved Bassinage water. Add half the salt water, squeeze and slap-fold for 2 minutes until absorbed, then add the remaining splash only if the dough feels cohesive and not soupy."
    durationMinutes: 8
  - step: 3
    title: "Four Coil Folds"
    text: "Every 30 minutes over the next 2 hours, wet both hands, reach under the center of the dough, lift it straight up until the ends release from the bowl, and tuck it down over itself like a coil. Rotate the bowl 90° and repeat 4 times. (If adding optional roasted garlic and cheddar, scatter evenly over the dough during Fold #2)."
    durationMinutes: 120
  - step: 4
    title: "Finish Bulk Fermentation to 60% Rise"
    text: "Leave the dough undisturbed after the 4th fold until the edges dome noticeably, small bubbles appear along the sides, and the dough has risen by 50% to 60% (do not wait for 100% doubling in Indian ambient heat, as overproofing will cause collapse)."
    durationMinutes: 45
    summerDurationMinutes: 45
    winterDurationMinutes: 75
  - step: 5
    title: "Pre-Shape, Final Tension Roll & Cold Retard"
    text: "Tip the dough onto a lightly floured counter, fold into a loose round, and let rest uncovered for 20 minutes to relax. Flip over, fold the sides inward, and roll tightly toward yourself against the counter friction to build a taut outer skin. Place seam-side up inside a colander or bowl lined with a cotton towel heavily dusted with Rice Flour (Chawal ka Atta). Seal inside a plastic bag and refrigerate at 4°C for 12 to 16 hours."
    durationMinutes: 20
  - step: 6
    title: "Two-Phase OTG Steam Bake"
    text: "Place an inverted heavy baking tray on the lowest rack slot and a metal tray on the OTG floor. Preheat OTG to 230°C (Both Rods On) for 20 minutes. Turn cold dough straight from the fridge onto parchment paper, dust off excess rice flour, and slash a 1.5cm deep curved arc from pole to pole at a 45° angle with a razor blade. Slide onto the hot tray, pour 1 cup boiling water into the floor tray, close door immediately, and switch OTG dial to Bottom Rod Only at 230°C for 18 minutes (Phase 1). After 18 minutes, remove the steam tray, switch to Both Rods On at 210°C, and bake for 18–20 minutes until deep mahogany (Phase 2). Cool on a wire rack for 1 hour before slicing."
    durationMinutes: 38
notes:
  - "The Open-Rack Two-Phase Secret: In tabletop OTGs (25L–60L), the top heating element sits very close to the loaf. Phase 1 (18 mins) uses Bottom Rod Only + boiling water steam tray to let the dough shoot upward without searing the top crust early. Phase 2 (20 mins) turns on Both Rods to blister the blistered mahogany crust."
  - "Bassinage Technique: Indian maida has a very fine particle size. Holding back 20ml water and splashing it in gradually with the salt allows the gluten network to absorb 75% hydration effortlessly."
  - "Rice Flour Rule: Always dust the proofing towel with Rice Flour (Chawal ka Atta), not maida. Rice flour contains no gluten and will never stick or tear your cold fermented dough."
