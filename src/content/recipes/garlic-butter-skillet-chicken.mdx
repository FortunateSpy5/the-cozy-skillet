instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Prep & Dry the Chicken"
    text: "Thoroughly pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels on both sides. Moisture prevents crispy browning. Season both sides evenly with salt and cracked pepper."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 2
    title: "Sear Skin-Side Down"
    text: "Set a 12-inch cast iron skillet or heavy bottom pan over medium flame. Add 1 tbsp oil. Once hot and shimmering, place chicken thighs skin-side down. Cook undisturbed for 10-12 minutes on medium flame. The skin will turn golden and crisp, releasing easily from the pan."
    durationMinutes: 11
  - step: 3
    title: "Flip and Add Aromatics"
    text: "Flip the chicken thighs. Drop in the butter, smashed garlic, and rosemary sprigs into the rendered pan juices. As the butter froths, tilt the pan slightly and spoon the aromatic garlic butter repeatedly over the chicken."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 4
    title: "Finish Cooking & Rest"
    text: "Lower flame to medium-low. Cook until internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F) at the thickest part near bone (about 6-8 minutes). Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the top, garnish with fresh herbs, and rest in the warm skillet 5 minutes before serving."
    durationMinutes: 8
