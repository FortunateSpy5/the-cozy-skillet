instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Prep Baking Sheets & True OTG Preheat"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 180°C (350°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On (Top + Bottom). Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper (or lightly grease with butter)."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Cream Butter, Sugar & Vanilla"
    text: "Place 200g softened butter, 200g castor sugar, and 0.5 tsp vanilla extract into a large mixing bowl. Beat vigorously with a wooden spoon or electric hand mixer for 2 to 3 minutes until pale, light, and fluffy. Gradually beat in the room-temperature egg until completely smooth and emulsified."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 3
    title: "Sift Dry Ingredients & Fold"
    text: "Sift 225g maida, 1 tsp baking soda, and 0.25 tsp salt directly over the creamed butter mixture. Using a spatula, fold gently just until no dry flour streaks remain. Do not overmix, or the cookies will turn tough rather than tender."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 4
    title: "Fold in Dual Chocolate Chips"
    text: "Add 115g white chocolate chips and 115g dark chocolate chips to the dough. Fold gently with a spatula until evenly distributed throughout the thick, buttery cookie dough."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 5
    title: "Portion onto Baking Sheets"
    text: "Drop heaped teaspoonfuls (or 1.5-tbsp cookie scoops) of dough onto the prepared baking sheets, leaving at least 2 inches of space between each dough mound to allow room for the cookies to spread during baking."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 6
    title: "Bake for the Golden-Edge Chewy Window"
    text: "Slide the baking sheet onto the middle rack of the preheated OTG. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 10 to 12 minutes. The cookies are ready when the edges are lightly golden and crisp, but the centers still look soft and slightly pale. Do not overbake!"
    durationMinutes: 11
  - step: 7
    title: "The 2-Minute Sheet Rest & Cool"
    text: "Remove the baking sheet from the OTG. Leave the cookies on the hot sheet undisturbed for 2 minutes—the residual heat completes carry-over cooking, setting the soft centers without drying them out. Transfer to a wire cooling rack to cool completely."
    durationMinutes: 2
notes:
  - "Butter Salt Balance: Standard Amul butter is salted (~2.5% salt). If using regular Amul butter, omit the added salt in the dough so the cookies maintain their pure buttery sweetness."
  - "The 10-Minute Pull Secret: Always remove cookies when the centers still look slightly underbaked. If baked until the entire center turns golden, the cookie will dry out into a hard biscuit once cool."
  - "Air Fryer Small-Batch Profile: Preheat air fryer to 160°C (320°F) for 3 minutes. Place 3 to 4 cookie dough mounds on a piece of perforated baking paper inside the basket. Bake at 160°C for 8 to 10 minutes until edges are golden."
  - "Giant Cast-Iron Skillet Cookie: Press the entire batch of dough into a buttered 10-inch cast iron skillet. Bake in the OTG at 175°C (347°F) for 22 to 25 minutes. Serve warm straight from the skillet topped with scoops of vanilla ice cream."
  - "Eggless Swap: Replace the 1 egg with 3 tbsp (45g) fresh whisked plain curd (dahi) or condensed milk."
  - "Storage: Store cooled cookies in an airtight biscuit tin with a slice of bread for up to 5 days; the bread keeps the cookies soft and chewy."
