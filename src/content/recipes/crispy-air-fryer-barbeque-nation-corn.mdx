instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Thaw & Pat Bone Dry"
    text: "If using frozen sweet corn, submerge in warm water for 5 minutes to thaw, then drain thoroughly in a colander (do NOT boil frozen corn, which waterlogs the cells). If using fresh raw corn, parboil in lightly salted boiling water for 3 to 4 minutes and drain. Spread the corn kernels in a single layer across clean kitchen towels or double-layered paper towels. Press and pat firmly until the kernels feel tacky to the touch and no pooled surface moisture remains."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 2
    title: "First Starch Seal"
    text: "Transfer the dry corn to a wide mixing bowl. Sprinkle with 3/4 tsp fine salt, 3/8 tsp black pepper, and 2 tbsp cornstarch. Toss with a spatula or your hands until every kernel is lightly coated. Let the bowl rest for 1 minute—this allows the cornstarch to absorb microscopic surface moisture and create an adhesive base."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 3
    title: "Double-Coat & Sift"
    text: "In a small cup, combine 2.5 tbsp cornstarch with 1.5 tbsp fine semolina (sooji). Sprinkle evenly over the corn and toss vigorously until each kernel is wrapped in an even, granular matte crust. Transfer the coated corn into a colander or fine-mesh sieve and gently shake over the sink to discard loose excess flour. Return the corn to the bowl and drizzle with 2.5 tbsp neutral cooking oil (or spray thoroughly), tossing until evenly glistening."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 4
    title: "Air Fry Batch 1 at 190°C"
    text: "Preheat your air fryer to 190°C (374°F) for 3 minutes. Spread half of the coated corn across the air fryer basket in a single loose layer (never overcrowd, as trapped steam prevents crisping). Air fry at 190°C for 13 to 15 minutes, pulling out the basket and giving it a vigorous shake every 4 minutes so all sides blister evenly to a deep golden crunch."
    durationMinutes: 14
  - step: 5
    title: "Air Fry Batch 2"
    text: "Tip the blistering Batch 1 corn into a large stainless steel or heatproof mixing bowl. Spread the remaining coated corn into the basket and air fry Batch 2 at 190°C for 13 to 15 minutes, shaking every 4 minutes until equally crisp and golden."
    durationMinutes: 14
  - step: 6
    title: "The Barbeque Nation Sizzle Toss"
    text: "Immediately combine both piping-hot batches in the wide mixing bowl. While the kernels are sizzling hot, immediately add the finely chopped raw red onion, minced green chilies, 1.5 tsp chaat masala, 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder, 1/2 tsp roasted coriander powder, optional coriander/spring onions, and 2.5 tbsp fresh lemon juice. Toss vigorously for 10 to 15 seconds so the spices bloom in the hot oil film and coat every single kernel. Serve immediately in bowls or a sizzling skillet."
    durationMinutes: 1
notes:
  - "The Sooji (Semolina) Secret: Pure cornstarch works well for deep frying, but in an air fryer with minimal oil, it can bake into a dull, chalky shell. Adding 1.5 tbsp fine sooji introduces microscopic granular ridges that puff and blister in high-velocity air, replicating the exact Barbeque Nation crunch."
  - "Moisture Control is Everything: Water is the mortal enemy of crispy corn. If the corn is wet when coated, the starch turns into a sticky batter clump, and trapped water can cause corn kernels to burst loudly. Patting thoroughly dry until tacky guarantees individual crisp kernels."
  - "Discard Excess Flour: Never skip sifting off the excess loose flour in Step 3. Loose starch that falls through the air fryer basket grates will burn on the bottom drawer and generate unwanted kitchen smoke."
  - "Stovetop Cast-Iron Kadai Alternative: If you don't own an air fryer, heat 3 to 4 tbsp neutral oil in a heavy cast-iron skillet or kadai over medium-high flame (180°C / 350°F). Add the coated corn, cover loosely with a splatter guard lid, and fry undisturbed for 3 minutes, then stir-fry for 4 to 5 minutes until blistered and golden. Drain on paper towels and proceed with the hot spice toss."
