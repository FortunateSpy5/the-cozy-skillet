instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Whisk the Discard Batter"
    text: "In a mixing bowl, combine 120g unfed sourdough discard, 25g melted (or browned) Amul butter, 2.5g salt, 3g roasted jeera + ajwain, 2g garlic powder, and optional grated cheese or crushed kasuri methi. Whisk until emulsified. Stir in 35g chakki atta until smooth and spreadable."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 2
    title: "Spread Paper-Thin on Parchment"
    text: "Place a fresh sheet of baking parchment directly on your counter. Dollop the mixture into the center and use an offset spatula (or the smooth flat back of a stainless steel katori) to spread it into a uniform paper-thin rectangle (approx 1.5mm thick, 9×12 inches). Sprinkle sesame seeds and flaky salt evenly over the surface, gently pressing them into the batter. Slide the parchment onto your OTG baking tray."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 3
    title: "Par-Bake & Score"
    text: "Slide the tray into your preheated 170°C (338°F) OTG (Both Rods On • Fan On, Middle Rack). Bake for 6 minutes until the surface feels just set and matte dry. Carefully slide the tray out, and immediately score into 1.5-inch squares using a pizza wheel or chef's knife."
    durationMinutes: 6
  - step: 4
    title: "Crisp to Golden & Cool"

Never discard sourdough starter in the sink—this 28-minute recipe transforms sour, fermented discard into shatteringly crisp Indian bakery thins. Infused with nutty browned Amul butter, roasted cumin (*jeera*), carom (*ajwain*), and garlic, these bake up with the snappy flakiness of an Irani cafe *khari* biscuit.
