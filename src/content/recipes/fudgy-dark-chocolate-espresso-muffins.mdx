instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Preheat OTG & Prep Muffin Pan"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 180°C (356°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On (Top + Bottom). Line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners. In a small bowl, toss 75g dark chocolate chips with 1 tsp flour to prevent sinking."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Whisk Dry Ingredients"
    text: "In a large mixing bowl, whisk together 185g maida, 200g sugar, 30g unsweetened cocoa powder, 5g baking soda, and 3g salt. Whisk vigorously for 1 minute to break up any stubborn cocoa clumps."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 3
    title: "Whisk Wet Ingredients"
    text: "In a second bowl, whisk together 240ml warm coffee, 15ml white vinegar, 80ml oil, 1 egg, and 10ml vanilla extract until cohesive and emulsified."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 4
    title: "Combine & Fold Batter"
    text: "Pour wet ingredients over dry ingredients. Whisk together gently just until combined and no dry streaks of flour remain (do not overmix; batter will be loose and slightly lumpy). Gently fold in 50g of the tossed chocolate chips with a spatula."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 5
    title: "Portion into Liners"
    text: "Divide batter evenly among the 12 muffin cups, filling each approximately 2/3 full. Scatter the reserved 25g chocolate chips over the top of each muffin."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 6
    title: "Bake in OTG"
    text: "Place muffin pan on the middle rack of your preheated OTG. Bake at 180°C (356°F) for 18 to 22 minutes (typically 20 minutes) until tops are set and a toothpick inserted into the center cake portion comes out clean (a little melted chocolate from chips is fine)."
    durationMinutes: 20
  - step: 7
    title: "Cool & 24-Hour Fudgy Maturation"
    text: "Transfer muffin pan to a wire rack. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then lift muffins out to cool completely to room temperature. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for 24 hours before serving. Chilling allows the starches and cocoa fats to mature into a dense, fudgy, truffle-like crumb."
    durationMinutes: 10
notes:
  - "Preheat Importance: Tabletop OTGs must be preheated for a full 15 minutes so the heating rods and metal chamber reach steady temperature before the muffin tin enters. Skipping preheat causes the baking soda reaction to stall, creating sunken, dense centers."
  - "100% Eggless Substitution: Swap the 1 egg for 3 tbsp (45g) of fresh whisked curd (dahi) or whole milk. The natural lactic acid in dahi complements the vinegar, producing an equally tender, bouncy crumb."
  - "Coffee Flavor: The warm coffee acts as an aromatic enhancer for the cocoa; the baked muffins do not taste like coffee, but rather have a rich, intensely dark chocolate flavor."
  - "Storage: Keeps in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 6 days. Serve cold for a dense fudge brownie texture, or microwave for 15 seconds for a warm molten center."
