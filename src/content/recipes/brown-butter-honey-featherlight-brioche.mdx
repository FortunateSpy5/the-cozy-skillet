instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Brown and Chill the Amul Butter"
    text: "Melt 100g Amul butter in a small skillet over medium-low flame. Swirl the pan gently as it foams. After 3 to 4 minutes, the milk solids toast into hazelnut-brown specks with a rich butterscotch aroma. Immediately pour butter and all toasted specks into a heatproof bowl. Chill in the refrigerator for 12 to 15 minutes until it returns to the consistency of soft room-temperature butter (scoopable, not liquid oil)."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Build the Strong Gluten Base First"
    text: "In a mixing bowl, combine warm milk, yeast, honey, eggs (or the eggless dahi-milk-butter blend), optional vanilla, maida, milk powder, and salt. Do not add the butter yet—early fat coats flour proteins and inhibits gluten development. Knead on a counter for 7 to 8 minutes until a coherent, elastic dough forms."
    durationMinutes: 8
  - step: 3
    title: "Emulsify the Softened Brown Butter"
    text: "Add the cooled brown butter in 4 small additions. Knead thoroughly for 2 to 3 minutes after each addition until the butter is completely incorporated before adding the next batch. The dough will become glossy, silky, and elastic, pulling cleanly away from the counter and passing the translucent windowpane test."
    durationMinutes: 10
  - step: 4
    title: "First Proof (Room Temp or Cold Ferment)"
    text: "Shape into a smooth ball. For same-day baking: let rise covered at room temperature for 60 minutes until doubled, then chill for 20 minutes in the fridge to firm the butter for easier shaping. For maximum flavor (Recommended): cover and refrigerate for 8 to 14 hours—slow cold fermentation develops unmatched French bakery depth."
    durationMinutes: 60
    summerDurationMinutes: 50
    winterDurationMinutes: 75
  - step: 5
    title: "Shape for Buns or Loaf & Second Proof"
    text: "Divide chilled dough into 8 equal portions for brioche burger buns / skillet rolls, or braid 3 strands and nestle into a greased 9x4-inch loaf tin. Cover loosely and proof at room temperature for 45 to 60 minutes until puffy and doubled."
    durationMinutes: 50
    summerDurationMinutes: 45
    winterDurationMinutes: 60
  - step: 6
    title: "Glaze & OTG Bake at 175°C (347°F) — Foil Tent Alert"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 175°C (347°F) with Both Rods On (Fan Off) for a full 15 minutes. Brush dough gently with egg wash (1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk) or eggless glaze (2 tbsp milk + 1 tsp honey). Because brioche is rich in honey, milk powder, and brown butter, place it on the lower-middle rack away from the top heating element. Bake for 22 to 25 minutes (for Loaf) or 16 to 18 minutes (for Burger Buns), tenting loosely with aluminium foil at the 12 to 14-minute mark to keep the crust silky and amber rather than over-browning. Cool 15 minutes before slicing."
    durationMinutes: 25
notes:
