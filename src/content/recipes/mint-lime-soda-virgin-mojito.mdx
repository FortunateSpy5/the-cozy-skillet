instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Assemble the Muddle Base"
    text: "In the bottom of a tall highball glass, add 1 tbsp freshly squeezed lime juice, 5 to 6 fresh mint leaves, 1 tsp sugar, and 1/8 tsp fine salt (plus optional ginger or black salt if making masala soda)."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 2
    title: "Gentle Muddle (Release the Menthol)"
    text: "Using the back of a wooden spoon or a cocktail muddler, press and twist down gently 4 to 5 times. The sugar crystals will gently bruise the mint leaves to release their fragrant menthol oils into the lime juice. (Do not over-muddle or shred the leaves, which releases bitter green chlorophyll tannins)."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 3
    title: "Add Ice & Lime Wheels"
    text: "Drop in 2 to 3 thin lime wheels against the inner glass walls, then fill the glass to the top with 4 to 5 large ice cubes."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 4
    title: "Pour Soda & Serve"
    text: "Slowly pour 240ml chilled Sprite or 7UP down the inside edge of the glass to preserve its effervescence. Give one gentle, single upward swirl with a long spoon to distribute the muddled mint throughout the glass. Garnish with a fresh mint sprig and enjoy ice-cold."
    durationMinutes: 1
notes:
  - "The Gentle Muddle Rule: Never crush or tear mint leaves into small shreds. Crushing breaks open bitter chlorophyll veins. Just 4–5 gentle presses release sweet menthol oils into the lime juice while keeping leaves whole."
  - "Sweetness Control: If you find standard Sprite too sweet, use 120ml Sprite + 120ml chilled unsweetened club soda (Kinley or Schweppes). It produces a sharper, more refreshing adult mocktail."
  - "Indian Street Twist: Adding 1/8 tsp black salt (kala namak) and a pinch of roasted cumin turns this into the beloved Shahi Masala Nimbu Soda served outside Delhi and Mumbai metro stations."
