instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Prep the Tin & True OTG Preheat"
    text: "Preheat your OTG to 210°C (410°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On (Top + Bottom). Take a large sheet of parchment paper, crumple it tightly into a compact ball under running water or dry in your hands, then shake it out and press it firmly into a 7-inch or 8-inch round cake tin. Let the wrinkled edges overhang the rim by 2 inches. The crumpling makes the stiff paper pliable and creates the rustic Basque pleated edge."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 2
    title: "Cream the Room-Temperature Cheese & Sugar"
    text: "Place 400g softened cream cheese and 110g castor sugar into a large mixing bowl. Beat with an electric hand mixer on low-medium speed for 2 minutes (or vigorously with a silicone spatula) until completely silky, glossy, and lump-free. Scrape down the sides of the bowl with a spatula."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 3
    title: "Beat in Eggs One at a Time"
    text: "Add the eggs one by one, beating on low speed just until each egg is fully incorporated before adding the next. Do not whip at high speed—excess air creates bubbles that will crack the custard unevenly."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 4
    title: "Stream in Cream, Flour & Aromatics"
    text: "Pour in 200ml room-temperature fresh cream, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 0.25 tsp salt, and optional lemon juice or espresso powder. Sift in 1 tbsp (10g) maida through a fine sieve. Whisk slowly on the lowest speed for 30 to 45 seconds until the batter is uniform, velvety, and flows like thick pancake batter."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 5
    title: "Pour, Tap & Settle"
    text: "Pour the silky batter into the prepared parchment-lined tin. Tap the tin firmly on your kitchen counter 3 to 4 times to release large air bubbles trapped inside the dense batter."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 6
    title: "High-Heat OTG Bake"
    text: "Place the tin on the middle rack (or lower-middle rack for 25L–30L OTGs) and bake at 210°C (410°F) for 35 to 40 minutes. The top will puff dramatically like a soufflé and turn a deep mahogany dark brown with caramelized scorched spots. When gently shaken with oven mitts, the edges should be set while the center 3 inches should jiggle prominently like set gelatin."
    durationMinutes: 38
  - step: 7
    title: "Cool at Room Temperature"
    text: "Transfer the tin to a wire cooling rack on your counter. Do not touch or slice! The puffed cheesecake will slowly collapse into a dense, flat crater as the steam escapes—this is completely normal. Allow it to cool undisturbed for 2 hours until fully room temperature."
    durationMinutes: 120
  - step: 8
    title: "Chill Firm & Slice"
    text: "Cover the tin loosely with foil and transfer to the refrigerator for at least 4 to 6 hours (preferably overnight). Once chilled, lift the cheesecake out of the tin using the overhanging parchment paper wings. Peel back the parchment, dip a sharp chef's knife into warm water, wipe dry, and slice clean restaurant-grade wedges."
    durationMinutes: 5
notes:
  - "Indian Cream Cheese Choice: D'lecta (block) and Britannia cream cheese tubs produce a gorgeously rich custard identical to imported Philadelphia. Ensure the cheese is fully softened at room temperature for 1 hour before creaming to avoid lumps."
  - "The Jiggle Test: Never overbake until the center is firm. If the center feels solid when you shake the pan, the molten custard core will turn into a dry cake. Trust the jiggle—it solidifies into pure silk in the fridge."
  - "Air Fryer Alternative: Preheat air fryer to 180°C (356°F) for 3 minutes. Line a 6-inch round tin with crumpled parchment, fill with batter, and air fry at 180°C for 22 to 25 minutes until deeply browned with a jiggly center."
  - "Storage: Keeps refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 5 days. For the ultimate molten texture, let slices sit at room temperature for 20 minutes before serving."
