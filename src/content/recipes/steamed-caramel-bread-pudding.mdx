instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Melt the Amber Caramel"
    text: "In a small heavy saucepan, add 1/2 cup (100g) sugar and 1 tbsp water. Place over low flame without stirring. As the sugar liquefies and bubbles, gently swirl the pan until it turns a rich golden amber color (watch closely; caramel can burn in seconds). Immediately pour the molten caramel into an ungreased 6-inch cake tin or steel tiffin box, swirling to coat the entire bottom. Set aside to cool and harden (takes 2–3 minutes)."
    durationMinutes: 4
  - step: 2
    title: "Blend the Bread Custard"
    text: "Tear the bread slices into rough chunks. In a blender jar (mixie), add the torn bread, 1 cup lukewarm milk, 2 eggs, 3 tbsp sugar, and 1/4 tsp vanilla essence (plus optional cardamom or condensed milk). Blend on medium speed for 45 to 60 seconds until completely smooth, silky, and free of any bread lumps."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 3
    title: "Pour & Seal with Foil"
    text: "Pour the smooth custard batter gently over the hardened caramel base in the cake tin. Cover the top of the tin tightly with aluminium foil, crimping the edges securely around the rim to prevent steam condensation from dripping onto the pudding."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 4
    title: "Setup the Steamer"
    text: "Place a metal trivet or ring in a deep kadai, steamer, or pressure cooker. Pour 2 cups (500ml) of water into the bottom and bring to a rolling boil over medium-high flame."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 5
    title: "Steam the Pudding"
    text: "Carefully lower the foil-wrapped cake tin onto the trivet. Cover the kadai with a tight lid (or close pressure cooker lid WITHOUT the whistle/weight). Reduce flame to medium-low and steam steadily for 25 to 30 minutes until the custard sets."
    durationMinutes: 28
  - step: 6
    title: "Check Doneness & Cool"
    text: "Turn off flame. Carefully peel back a corner of the foil and insert a clean knife or toothpick into the center of the pudding. It should come out clean without any wet batter sticking. Let the tin cool completely on the counter for 30 minutes."
    durationMinutes: 5
  - step: 7
    title: "Chill & Invert"
    text: "Transfer the cooled tin to the refrigerator and chill for at least 2 hours (or overnight). To unmold, gently run a thin knife around the inner rim. Place an inverted rimmed serving plate over the tin, hold firmly, and flip over. Tap the base gently; the pudding will glide out with a cascade of amber caramel sauce. Slice into wedges and serve chilled."
    durationMinutes: 5
notes:
  - "The Blender Secret: Blending bread into the custard rather than soaking and mashing with a fork creates a velvet-smooth, lump-free texture identical to classic restaurant crème caramel."
  - "Steamer Choice: If using an Indian pressure cooker, make sure to completely remove the whistle/weight. The gentle steady steam without high pressure cooks the custard evenly without curds or bubbles."
  - "OTG Water Bath (Bain-Marie) Alternative: If baking in an OTG instead of steaming, preheat OTG to 170°C (338°F) for 15 minutes with Both Rods On. Place the tightly foil-sealed cake tin into a deep roasting pan, pour boiling water into the pan until halfway up the sides of the tin, and bake on the middle rack for 35 to 40 minutes until set."
  - "Crusts Option: Trimming crusts off the bread produces a classic pale ivory custard body; keeping crusts adds golden color and a subtle toasty note."
  - "Storage: Keeps refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 4 days. The caramel will continue to liquidize into a delicious dipping sauce."
