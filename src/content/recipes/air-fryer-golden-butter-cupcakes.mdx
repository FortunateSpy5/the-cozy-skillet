instructions:
  - step: 1
    title: "Beat Butter & Sugar"
    text: "In a large mixing bowl, beat 170g butter and 110g powdered sugar using a hand whisk or electric beater for 2 minutes until pale, light, and fluffy."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 2
    title: "Add Eggs One by One"
    text: "Add the 3 eggs one by one, beating thoroughly for 30 seconds after each addition until fully incorporated and emulsified. Stir in 1 tsp vanilla essence and 3 tbsp milk."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 3
    title: "Whisk Dry Ingredients"
    text: "In a separate bowl, whisk together 185g maida, 1.5 tsp baking powder, and 1/4 tsp salt. If using chocolate chips, toss 50g chips with 1 tsp of this dry flour mix in a small bowl."
    durationMinutes: 1
  - step: 4
    title: "Fold Batter"
    text: "Add the dry ingredients to the creamed wet mixture. Using a spatula, fold gently in a sweeping circular motion just until no dry flour streaks remain (do not overmix, or cupcakes will become dense). Gently fold in the optional chocolate chips."
    durationMinutes: 2
  - step: 5
    title: "Fill Moulds & Preheat"
    text: "Preheat your Air Fryer to 160°C (320°F) for 3 minutes (or preheat your OTG to 175°C (347°F) for 15 minutes). Spoon batter into silicone cupcake moulds or rigid paper cups, filling each 2/3 full. Arrange a maximum of 4 moulds in the air fryer basket with space between them."
    durationMinutes: 3
  - step: 6
    title: "Air Fry in Batches (or Bake in OTG)"
    text: "Bake in the air fryer at 160°C (320°F) for 14 to 15 minutes. Check with a toothpick in the center; it should come out clean with no wet batter. Carefully remove the batch, load the next 4 moulds, and repeat for remaining batter. (If using an OTG, bake all 12 on the middle rack at 175°C (347°F) for 18–20 minutes)."
    durationMinutes: 15
  - step: 7
    title: "Cool & Enjoy"
    text: "Transfer cupcakes to a wire cooling rack. Let cool for 10 minutes before removing from silicone moulds. Enjoy warm, or store in an airtight container for up to 3 days."
    durationMinutes: 10
notes:
  - "Air Fryer Capacity Note: Most Indian 3.5L to 5.5L air fryer baskets (Philips, Instant Pot, Agaro) hold exactly 4 silicone moulds with enough clearance for radial air convection. Baking 4 at a time ensures rapid 15-minute domes without uneven browning."
  - "Butter State: If using melted butter, allow it to cool to room temperature before beating with sugar. Hot butter will melt the sugar crystals and prevent aeration, leading to a flatter, oily crumb."
  - "OTG Alternative: If you prefer to bake the entire batch at once, bake in a standard 12-cup lined muffin tray on the middle rack of a preheated OTG at 175°C (347°F) for 18 to 20 minutes."
